Supplier health for travel APIs.

Look-to-book, latency and errors in one verdict. Check a supplier in seconds, rank a whole portfolio from a pasted table, and send the result to your team as one line.

Runs in your browser. Your numbers never leave your device.

What it measures

Three numbers decide whether a supplier connection is healthy. The worst of the three sets the verdict.

Look-to-book

Searches sent per confirmed booking. The number suppliers watch, cap and sometimes invoice.

Read the guide

Latency

How long the supplier takes to answer. Averages hide the slow tail that customers actually feel.

Read the guide

Error rate

Failed calls as a share of all calls. Technical failures and business rejections need to be counted apart.
