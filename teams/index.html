Teams

APIPulse for teams

APIPulse is free and runs in a single browser today. If several people in your company look after supplier connections, these are the things we are exploring next. Nothing here is for sale yet.

Shared supplier registry

One list of suppliers, their profiles and their agreed limits, shared by the whole integration and supply team instead of living in each person's browser.

Contract limits as settings

Look-to-book caps and response-time commitments stored per supplier, with the period and definition each agreement uses.

Period comparison

Which suppliers got worse since last week or last month, and on which measure.

Alerts and weekly reports

A message in Slack or e-mail when a supplier crosses a limit, and a short weekly summary for supplier reviews.

Data from your logs

Counts and response times taken from logs or an endpoint you control, so nobody has to paste numbers by hand.
